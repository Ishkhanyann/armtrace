# ArmTrace

Clothing supply-chain traceability for manufacturers, logistics operators, controllers and consumers.

## Language

**Batch / Խմբաքանակ**:
A quantity of clothing with the same article, color and size, tracked together through the supply chain under one QR code.
_Avoid_: Individual item, shipment.

**Manufacturer / Արտադրող**:
The organization that produces clothing and registers its batches in the supply chain.

**Logistics operator / Լոգիստիկ օպերատոր**:
The participant who records the receipt, movement or handover of goods.

**Controller / Վերահսկող**:
The participant who monitors the supply chain and manages participant access.

**Consumer / Սպառող**:
The person who views the origin, route and current status of clothing.
